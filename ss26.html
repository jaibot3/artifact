2026 Spring–Summer Collection

The 26’ SPRING–SUMMER collection begins with a sense of release—an easing of tension that emerges when one steps into nature. Observing the textures revealed by sunlight, the subtle movements shaped by the wind, and the delicate patterns left on sand, these sensory impressions are translated into garments.

Rooted in the emotional flow of nature and a sense of inner ease, the collection deepens simplicity with subtle complexity, expanding AMOMENTO’s quietly distinctive design language. The nuanced contrasts created by varied textures and techniques bring richness to otherwise minimal silhouettes, proposing a sense of effortless calm within everyday life.
